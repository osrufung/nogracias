Why 400?

Since 17 October 2026, sales calls in Spain must come from numbers starting with 400, so you can spot them at a glance, and «No, gracias» spots them for you. What the rules say.

How it works

  1. Install the app and open it.
  2. Turn it on in Settings › Apps › Phone › Call Blocking & Identification.
  3. Choose identify or block. Change it whenever you like from the app, Control Center, Siri or Shortcuts.
